This journal records how my understanding and the four projects developed between 7 September and 26 October 2026. Each entry covers what I did, what I learned, what was difficult and what came next. Entries link to the relevant project, section or appendix page.

Overview

My understanding moved from fitting models to checking them. Early on I was identifying ARIMA structure and benchmarking forecasts. The Project 2 work taught me that a result needs a proper null distribution, since I had to simulate the maximum of the coherence before I could judge my peak. In October the hand-coded estimators (IRLS, the Kalman filter, Baum-Welch) made validation against a known answer a habit. By the end I was spending more time on what the data could support than on which model fitted best.
