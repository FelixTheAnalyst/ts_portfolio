A Student-t GARCH(1,1) with a lagged Google Trends term in the variance equation, compared with GARCH, GJR and EGARCH benchmarks. Evaluated in sample (boundary-corrected likelihood ratio) and out of sample (QLIKE against realised variance), with a power study.
